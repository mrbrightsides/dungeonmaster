## Part 2: Character Creation & World Setup (0:30 - 1:00)
**[Visual: Select "Obsidian Crags", "Grimdark" tone, and the "Mage" class. Click to Start.]**

**Speaker:** "We’re starting in the Obsidian Crags with a Grimdark tone. Notice the UI immediately adapts. Our custom Visualizer component uses CSS particles and dynamic SVGs to reflect the environment—in this case, floating embers and dark volcanic peaks."

**Speaker:** "The Dungeon Master greets us. Using the Gemini 2.5 Flash TTS model, we’ve given the DM a gravelly, cinematic voice that reads the narrative as it’s typed out on screen, creating an immersive, tabletop-like experience."

## Part 3: Dynamic Gameplay & Function Calling (1:00 - 2:00)
**[Visual: Type "I cautiously investigate the glowing runes on the cave wall" into the action bar]**

**Speaker:** "Let’s interact with the world. I’ll investigate these runes. Behind the scenes, we aren't just getting text back. We are using Gemini 3's advanced reasoning and Function Calling."

**[Visual: Show a Quest appearing in the sidebar or a Stat change occurring]**

**Speaker:** "The AI decided my investigation was successful. It triggered a 'Quest Update' tool, giving me a new objective and boosting my XP. Everything you see—the items in my backpack, the health of the enemies, even the biome transitions—is managed by a strictly typed JSON state returned by the model."

**[Visual: An enemy spawns. Type "I unleash a torrent of arcane fire at the skeleton"]**

**Speaker:** "Combat is where the 'Visualizer' shines. When I attack, the AI calculates the outcome. You’ll see the screen shake, chromatic aberration effects, and floating damage numbers. These aren't pre-rendered; they are React-driven animations triggered by the AI's logic."
